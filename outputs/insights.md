## Findings

1. **Festive season drives sales.** October and November together bring in 21% of yearly sales, so stock and marketing budgets should be planned around this period.
2. **Electronics is the biggest category** with 65% of sales, but **Clothing** has the best profit margin (43.5%).
3. **Deep discounts wipe out profit.** Orders at 20% or more discount have a margin of 3.7%, compared with 21.2% for orders below 20%, and 30% discounts are loss-making.
4. **West is the top region** (₹1.41 Cr), while **Central** is the smallest (₹46.93 L), which points to a growth opportunity.
5. **Weakest sub-category by profit:** Paper (Office Supplies) with total profit of ₹2.08 L.
